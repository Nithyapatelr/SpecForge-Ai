Hospital Appointment Management System — Sample Requirements

Functional Requirements

1. The system shall allow registered patients to search for available appointment slots by selecting a medical specialty, preferred doctor, and date range.
2. When a patient books an appointment, the system shall send a confirmation notification to both the patient and the assigned doctor within 60 seconds.
3. The system shall allow patients to cancel or reschedule an appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time without any penalty.
4. If a patient fails to cancel within the 24-hour window, the system shall record a no-show event and notify the billing module to apply a late-cancellation fee.
5. An appointment moves from the state 'Requested' to 'Confirmed' once a doctor or receptionist accepts it; it moves to 'Cancelled' if either party withdraws before the appointment time; and it moves to 'Completed' once the doctor marks the visit as done.
6. Only users assigned the 'Receptionist' or 'Doctor' role shall be permitted to modify another patient's appointment on their behalf.
7. The patient date-of-birth field must be stored in ISO 8601 format (YYYY-MM-DD) and must not be a future date.
8. The system shall integrate with the Twilio SMS API to send appointment reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the scheduled time; if Twilio is unavailable the system shall retry twice before logging the failure.
9. The appointment booking page must load within 2 seconds for 95 percent of requests under a simulated load of 100 concurrent users.
10. The system should respond quickly to all user interactions.
11. The system shall provide a user-friendly interface that non-technical staff can operate without training.
12. Only a Senior Doctor may approve prescriptions for Schedule II controlled substances; a General Practitioner may approve all other prescriptions.
13. Each prescription record must contain: prescription_id (UUID), patient_id (UUID), doctor_id (UUID), medication_name (string, max 255 characters), dosage (string), and issue_date (ISO 8601 date).
14. The system shall export patient appointment history as a PDF report and shall send it to the patient's registered email address upon request.
15. The report generation for a 12-month appointment history must complete in under 5 seconds when the patient has fewer than 500 appointments on record.
